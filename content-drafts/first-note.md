The smallest ideas can be the easiest to explain. Send a message without saving a contact. Share a link without a string of tracking parameters. Give a young learner a friendly place to explore.

These are the starting points behind three of my personal apps.

## A conversation without a new contact

[Unknown Messenger](https://programmercat.com/apps/) removes a step before a WhatsApp conversation. It is built for the moments when you need to send a message without adding someone to your address book.

## A cleaner link to share

[Clean My Links](https://programmercat.com/apps/) tackles another small annoyance: URLs filled with campaign tags and tracking parameters. Its job is to make the link you share a little tidier.

## A space for little learners

[Minku Learns](https://programmercat.com/apps/) brings early learning activities into an app for children aged three to five. Letters, numbers, and words provide the starting point for exploration.
