I have worked at PayPal since August 2021. I joined as a Software Engineer 3 and became a Senior Software Engineer in April 2025.

I currently lead a small team building a multi-tenant UK payment initiation system on Faster Payments rails, from requirements and architecture through bank integrations, consent, secure money movement, settlement, and reconciliation.

In separate work I helped design an aggregator switching service, a merchant payment-method eligibility API, and a simulator and testing library that makes functional testing faster. I have also worked on payment integrations and settlement pipelines, including the reliability and reconciliation work that comes with them. The code is mostly Java and Spring services backed by Oracle.
