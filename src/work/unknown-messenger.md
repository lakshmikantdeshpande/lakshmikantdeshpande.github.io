## For the one-off conversation

A delivery update. A quick enquiry. A message to someone you may only need to contact once.

Unknown Messenger helps you start a WhatsApp conversation without first saving the phone number to your contacts.

## Less setup, same conversation

The app handles the step before the chat. Enter a number and move into the conversation, without filling your address book with temporary contacts.

It is a focused Android utility, built around a small piece of friction that is easy to recognise once you have encountered it.

## A useful kind of small

Not every project needs a long feature list. Sometimes the most helpful thing an app can do is shorten an everyday task.
