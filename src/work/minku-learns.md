## A little room for discovery

Minku Learns is a learning companion for children aged three to five. It brings letters, numbers, and words into a space made for young learners.

The idea is simple: give children something they can explore, with activities that fit into the small moments of their day.

## Built around the learner

A children's app asks different questions of its design. Is the next step clear? Are the interactions forgiving? Can a child explore without being overwhelmed?

Those questions shape Minku Learns. The focus is on approachable activities and a friendly experience, with learning at the centre.

## Small app, meaningful problem

Minku Learns is part of my collection of personal apps: focused tools for everyday use, built alongside my work on backend systems.
